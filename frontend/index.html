📦 W2R Process — Cardboard & Plastic Collection

When: Weekend services (Saturday & Sunday) and special events like GPC (5 consecutive days in July).

What: Flatten cardboard boxes and collect plastic water bottles from designated collection points.

How to log it: Open the Telegram bot → "🪣 Recycling Logs" → choose Cardboard or Plastic → pick a date (Today, or type a past date to back-add) → send a photo, enter the weight, repeat for each measurement if the load was weighed in parts → review the breakdown → Confirm. Everything syncs to the portal automatically.

⚠️ Exact bin/collection point locations and weighing scale procedure — pending write-up.

⚡ Energy Tracking — What the numbers mean

The Energy tab tracks monthly electricity (kWh) and water (m³) usage. Selecting a "Big Event" pill (e.g. GPC, Youth Camp) shows how that period compared to the average monthly baseline, with the exact % difference on hover.

⚠️ The High/Medium/Low impact labels shown on event pills are a working classification — formal kWh/m³ thresholds for what counts as high vs. medium vs. low are still being defined.

🔄 Roster, Priority & Swaps

Priority tags in Member Profiles: ✅ Serve Next = prioritise for the next roster, ⚠️ Soon = include if slots are available, — Rest = served recently, give others a turn.

Requesting a swap: In the bot, tap Roster → Request Swap → choose your rostered duty from the list → give a reason → confirm. The ministry group is notified automatically.

📢 Comms Workflow

Posts move through four stages: Idea → Draft → Planned → Posted. Drafts are shared in the group chat for team comments before Judy posts the final version.

Once something goes live, mark it "✅ Mark posted" on the Comms tab to archive it — this can be reverted from the archive list if it was marked by mistake.

🤖 Using the Telegram Bot

Tap /start on the bot to register — you'll be asked a few quick profile questions the first time (service day, CG, other ministries, birthday). After that, the main menu gives you Roster, Recycling Logs, and (as features roll out) Stats, Profile, and Availability.

Questions or something not working? Message a Team Lead directly.
